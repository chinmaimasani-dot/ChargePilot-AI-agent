AC AND DC CHARGING BASICS

AC charging (alternating current) uses the vehicle's onboard charger to
convert AC power to DC. It is slower but gentler on the battery, making it
suitable for overnight or long-duration parking. Typical AC power levels
range from 3.3 kW to 22 kW.

DC charging (direct current, also called fast charging) bypasses the
onboard charger and feeds DC power directly to the battery. It is much
faster, usually adding a significant charge in 30-60 minutes, but frequent
DC fast charging can increase battery wear over time.

For daily use, prefer AC charging and reserve DC fast charging for long
trips or when time is limited.
