CONNECTOR TYPES AND SAFETY

Common connector types in India include:
- CCS2 (Combined Charging System): the standard for DC fast charging on
  most modern EVs.
- Type 2 (Mennekes): the standard for AC charging on most modern EVs.
- Bharat AC-001 and Bharat DC-001: older public chargers for early EVs.
- CHAdeMO: used by some older EV models.

Safety guidelines:
- Never touch a connector pin; hold the plug body only.
- Stop charging before unplugging.
- Do not use a charging cable that is visibly damaged.
- Report any burning smell, unusual noise or smoke, and move away from the
  charger immediately.
