DC CHARGER MAINTENANCE GUIDELINES

DC fast chargers require periodic inspection of the power module cooling
system. Check coolant levels monthly and replace coolant every 12 months.
Inspect the charging cable and connector for cracks, burns or deformation
before every shift. Clean the connector pins only with a dry, lint-free
cloth; never use water or solvent inside the connector housing.

Cabinet air filters must be cleaned every two weeks in dusty environments.
Tighten all power terminals during the quarterly inspection. Firmware
updates should be applied during maintenance windows, never during peak
usage hours.

If a DC charger trips repeatedly, log the fault code from the display and
contact the operations desk. Do not restart a charger more than twice.
