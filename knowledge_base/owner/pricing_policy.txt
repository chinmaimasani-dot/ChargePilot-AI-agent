PRICING AND OPERATIONAL POLICY

Charging prices are set per kWh and can differ between AC and DC chargers
and between locations. Owners may update prices at any time through the
assistant; the new price applies to sessions started after the change.

Simulated revenue reporting counts only completed charging sessions in the
selected date range. Cancelled or failed sessions are excluded from revenue
but remain visible in session history.

Marking a charger unavailable stops new sessions but does not affect
sessions already in progress.
